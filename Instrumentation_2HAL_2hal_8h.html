Detailed Description

Instrumentation hardware interface: CAN bus plus the five I2C sensors on the board:

  • SHT45 (temp/humidity)
  • ADS1115 (4-channel ADC)
  • SCD41 (CO2)
  • MS5611 (pressure)
  • FSP201 (accelerometer)
